The predicted effect of motion coherence on mean PMT in RS blocks is more complex, and depends on the mechanism that governs the transition between the first phase of evidence accumulation and sustained activity at the motor preparation level. Based on our literature review in the previous section, two processing schemes can be dissociated. If, on every trial, motor preparation accumulation trajectories reach the same level of sustained activity, then the starting point of motor preparation for the second phase of evidence accumulation should be the same across motion coherence levels. Consequently, mean PMT should not be modulated by motion coherence (Figure 1B; Prediction 5a).
